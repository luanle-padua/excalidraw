Part 6 · Around the Meeting

Settings & profile

One window holds everything you can change about your own account and this device: your display name and avatar, your read-only organisation details, your password, every meeting preference, the data we keep, and your microphone consent. Open it from the meeting header gear or the dashboard account menu, and every control here behaves exactly like its inline twin.

Six tabs, one window

Account settings is the single place to manage yourself. It opens as a Glass-Desk panel titled Account settings, with a tab rail down the left and the chosen tab's controls on the right. A close button labelled Close sits top-right, and pressing Esc — or clicking the dimmed area outside the card — dismisses the whole window.

The rail shows up to six tabs: Profile, Account, Security, Preferences, My data, and Privacy. Which tab you land on depends on how you opened the window — clicking your own avatar in the participants bar, or the "set up your profile" nudge, jumps straight to Profile; the gear and the account menu open it the same way. Each time the window opens it re-seeds from your stored profile, so a half-finished edit you abandoned never carries over.

Guests

If you joined by magic link rather than a password, the Security tab is hidden — there is no password to change. Magic-link logins are also a no-op for the parts of My data they cannot read; a list you have no access to simply shows its empty state rather than an error.

Anatomy
  1. 1 Window title — Account settings.
  2. 2 Close button (top-right X). Esc or an outside click does the same.
  3. 3 Tab rail: Profile, Account, Security, Preferences, My data, Privacy.
  4. 4 The active tab's panel — controls render on the right.
  5. 5 Profile preview: avatar, display name, and company, updating live as you type.

Set your name, company, and avatar

  1. Open Account settings and select the Profile tab (you usually land here).
  2. Type into Display name — placeholder e.g. Luan Le, up to 48 characters. The live preview and the Save button enable as soon as it is non-blank.
  3. Optionally fill Company — placeholder e.g. MAP Architects, also up to 48 characters.
  4. Pick a built-in tile from the Avatar gallery, or press ⬆ Upload image to choose your own picture.
  5. Press Save. The window stays open; there is no Cancel button here — the header Close is the dismiss.

→ Your new name, company, and avatar save to this device and broadcast to everyone in the meeting — your participant tile, cursor label, and the built-in avatar list all update at once. A library avatar (a lib: pick) also syncs to your account, so it follows your login to other browsers; an uploaded image stays on this device only.

Note

Display name is required: with the field blank the preview shows the placeholder and Save stays disabled. Leading and trailing spaces are trimmed before saving.

Host-only

Nothing here renames or removes other people — this tab only ever edits your own identity. Host controls for moderating participants live in the meeting, not in Settings.

Three ways to set your avatar

Eight built-in tiles. Click one to select it (a selected ring appears); it is stored as a lib: reference and syncs to your account across devices.

⬆ Upload image opens a file picker (images only). The picture is auto-resized to 256 px on its longest edge and kept on this device. A Remove button appears once an avatar is set.

With no avatar chosen — or after pressing Remove — your tile falls back to a generated avatar built from your name and email.

Use a gallery tile if you want the same face on every device you sign in from; upload an image when you want your real photo, accepting that it stays local to this browser.

Change your password, or sign out

  1. Open the Security tab (password logins only — guests don't see it).
  2. Type your existing password into Current password.
  3. Type the new one into New password, then again into Confirm new password.
  4. Press Save password. The button reads Saving… while it works.
  5. On success a green banner reads Password changed. and the three fields clear.

→ Your current password is verified first by re-authenticating, then the new one is set — so even on an already-signed-in browser nobody can change your password without knowing the old one.

Note

New passwords must be at least 8 characters (New password must be at least 8 characters.) and the two new fields must match (Confirmation doesn't match.). A wrong current password gives Current password is incorrect.; a password that fails policy (or equals the old one) shows the server's reason or Couldn't change password. Try again. Save password stays disabled until all three fields have text. The Sign out button at the bottom ends your session immediately.

Microphone consent and what we keep

The Privacy tab gives you a single, plain-language consent switch and a note describing the data the app holds. The toggle is labelled Allow mic transcription (Deepgram): when on, your mic audio is sent to Deepgram for live transcription; when off, nothing is sent.

This is the same on/off state as Enable mic for STT in Preferences — flipping it here also flips it there, and vice versa. Turning it off is an immediate, complete opt-out: no audio leaves your device for transcription.

What we store about you

The note reads: We store your profile (name, company, avatar), a log of meetings you join, files you upload, and transcripts while you have STT on. Interface preferences (language, theme, captions) are kept on this device only. Your My data tab lets you review the lists behind this — your meetings, invitations, and files — each read-only, with empty states (No meetings yet., No invitations yet., No files yet.) when there is nothing to show and Loading… while they fetch.

Settings troubleshooting

Save on the Profile tab is greyed out — Display name is blank (or only spaces).

Type a non-blank display name; Save enables once it has real text. → 16.4

No Security tab in the rail — You signed in by magic link, which has no password.

Expected for guests. Password changes only apply to password logins. → 16.7

Account fields all show a dash and a yellow banner — The org record lookup failed; the panel fell back to your session.

Reopen later; the banner reads Couldn't load account info… and your verified session values still appear. → 16.6

Background picker is greyed out — You're on iPad or phone web, where background effects aren't supported.

Use a desktop browser; the note Background effects are only supported on desktop explains the limit. → 16.8

High video-quality tiers are dim and unclickable — An admin lowered the org-wide max video-quality cap.

Pick a tier at or below the cap, or Auto. The note names the cap. → 16.8

Uploaded avatar isn't on my other device — Uploaded images are kept on this device only; only gallery picks sync.

Choose a built-in gallery tile to have an avatar follow your login. → 16.5

Only image files are accepted. after uploading — The chosen file wasn't an image.

Pick a picture file (PNG, JPG, etc.). → 16.5

Note

Closing the window discards nothing already saved — preference toggles and password changes commit the moment you make them; only an unsaved Profile edit is dropped, and reopening re-seeds the stored values.
