IFC building models

Bring a BIM model onto the board and spin it up — in place on the canvas, or in the full 3D view with storeys, sections, measure and render styles.

Spin a model up without leaving the canvas

  1. Right-click an IFC anchor and choose Edit 3D (rotate / zoom) to make it live where it sits — drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, click an element to select it. (Choose Open in 3D view for the full pane instead — next.)
  2. The inline toolbar carries the same essentials as the pane: ↻ Fit, 🏢 Storeys, ✂️ Section, 📏 Measure, 👻 Ghost and 🎨 Style.
  3. ↻ Refresh re-renders the angle you are on in high resolution and re-syncs it (tooltip “Re-render this view in high resolution and re-sync it”).
  4. × Exit (or Esc) bakes the viewpoint you leave into the anchor and shares it, so the whole room sees the model from the same angle.

→ The camera angle and the picture on the canvas are shared; your live orbiting, sections, measurements and any elements you hide stay on your screen until you exit.

Browsing every object

The object tree — where you hide and show individual elements — lives in the full 3D view pane. The inline canvas editor gives you the camera, section and measure tools, but not the tree.

Open a building model in 3D view

  1. Click the 3D view button in the canvas toolbar (the cube icon, tooltip “3D view — open IFC model in a pane”).
  2. The popover IFC in library lists every model; pick one to open it as a tab marked 🧊 in the right-docked pane.
  3. Or right-click an IFC anchor on the canvas and choose Open in 3D view.
  4. Once it loads, drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, and click any element to select it and open its properties.
  5. Use Fit to reframe the whole model whenever you lose your bearings.
  6. Resize the pane from its left edge, switch models via the tabs, and close with the tab × or the pane ×.

→ Clicking an element opens a properties panel listing its Name, Type, Category, Family, and Type name, plus any extra Properties the model carries. Close it with its × (Deselect).

Note

While the model is still loading you will see “Loading 3D model…”. Every toolbar button stays greyed out until the model is ready.

3D view toolbar
  1. 1 Fit — frames the whole model and resets the camera (“Fit model to pane”).
  2. 2 Storeys — lists the building's floors by elevation. Click one to isolate it; click All to show every storey again. The badge shows the floor count.
  3. 3 Objects — the object tree, grouped storey → category → element. Expand a branch, click an element to select and frame it, or use the eye toggle (👁 / 🚫) to hide and show it. Show all storeys clears any isolation.
  4. 4 View style — a three-way switch: Shaded, Clay, Wireframe (see the gallery below).
  5. 5 Section — the scissors group with X, Y, Z cut axes. Pick an axis, drag the plane in the 3D frame to slice; Flip swaps which half is kept, and the eye button hides or shows the cut plane while keeping the slice. Click the active axis again to turn the section off.
  6. 6 Measure — click two points on the model to read the distance; the result appears as 📏 with two decimals. Click Measure again to stop.
  7. 7 Ghost — fades everything except your current selection so a single element stands out.

Three ways to render the model

Shows each element's original colour — the realistic default.

Overrides everything with a uniform grey, so form reads without colour noise.

Shows edge lines only — useful for seeing through the model.

Switch to Clay for clean massing discussions, Wireframe to inspect structure behind a facade, and back to Shaded when colour matters.
