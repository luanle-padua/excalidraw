Part 6 · Around the Meeting

Leaving & review

Every meeting has a way out, and most have an afterlife. This chapter covers the difference between hanging up your call, walking out of the room, and the moment a host ends the meeting for everyone — and what a finished meeting lets you do when you open it again to read the record.

Three ways to step out — and they are not the same

Canvas M deliberately gives leaving three distinct verbs, each with its own glyph in the header, so you never confuse a small step back with shutting the whole room down.

Leave call (a hang-up handset in the media group) only drops you out of the live audio/video — you stay in the meeting, the canvas is still live, and you can rejoin the call with Join. Leave (an arrow-out-of-box icon, far right) exits the meeting room entirely: your canvas is saved, the room is cleared from the URL so you won't auto-rejoin on reload, and you land back on your project home. Everyone else stays. End meeting (a red power icon) is the host-only kill switch — it finishes the meeting for everyone at once.

Leave call = hang up. Leave = walk out. End meeting = power off the room.
The three exit glyphs Left to right: hang-up (Leave call), arrow-out (Leave), red power (End meeting).
Host-only

The red End meeting button only appears for the host, organiser, co-host, or a project authority. If you don't see it, you can't end the meeting for everyone — and that's by design. The server enforces the same rule, so the button's absence is not the only guard.

Leaving the meeting

  1. Find the Leave button at the far-right edge of the header (the arrow-out-of-box icon).
  2. Click it. There's no confirmation prompt — leaving is a safe, reversible action.
  3. Your canvas is saved automatically before the connection closes.
  4. The room is removed from the address bar, so a reload won't pull you back in.
  5. You return to your project home, where the meeting may still be offered as Resume meeting if it's still running.

→ You're out of the room. The meeting carries on for everyone who stayed, and you can re-open it any time it's still live.

Note

If the host removes you from the meeting, you'll see a notice and then be taken out the same way — you don't have to click Leave yourself.

When the host ends the meeting

Ending is the host's call, and it's final. When the host clicks End meeting, they first confirm the prompt “End the meeting for EVERYONE? All participants switch to read-only review.” — and that's exactly what happens to you.

Within a second or two, your meeting flips into review mode without dropping you. The canvas becomes read-only, a banner appears across the top, and the meeting is marked finished. You don't get kicked out — you can stay and read the record, download the transcript, or step out with Leave whenever you're ready. Behind the scenes the meeting is recorded as finished so that re-opening it later always lands you back in this same read-only review.

Good to know

Ending also kicks off an automatic AI recap from the transcript and chat — it runs in the background and never delays the meeting from ending. You'll find that recap surfaced later in the meeting's detail view.

What review mode locks — and what it leaves open

A status banner reads “Reviewing (read-only) · meeting finished — extract only” with an eye icon, pinned to the canvas so you always know the meeting is frozen.

The conversation panel shows “Reviewing a finished meeting — chat is read-only”. You can read every message but can't send new ones.

Uploads are disabled: “The meeting has finished — the library is read-only, uploads are disabled.” Existing files stay viewable.

You'll see all three together the moment a meeting finishes — whether the host ended it live, or you opened a finished meeting from your portal or project list. Content-creating tools (stickers, the canvas bot, text translation) are hidden, and you can't present or edit the meeting's title.

Reading the transcript & generating a summary

  1. Open the log from the header's Transcript button (the document icon — it shows a count badge when there's a transcript).
  2. The log opens on the Transcript tab: the full conversation, grouped by speaker with avatars, timestamps, and a per-line language tag.
  3. Switch to the Summary tab. If none exists yet, click Generate summary — Gemini reads the full transcript and returns an overview, participants, key topics, decisions, and action items.
  4. Watch for “Generating summary… (~5-15s for long meetings)”; a dot appears on the Summary tab once it's ready.
  5. Click Download .md to save the current tab (transcript or summary) as a Markdown file.
  6. Press Esc or click outside the modal to close it.

→ You've read the record and, if you wanted, pulled a structured recap and a downloadable copy — without changing anything in the finished meeting.

Host-only

In a live meeting the footer also offers Clear log (wipes the transcript and summary for the room after a confirm) and Regenerate summary. Both are hidden in review — a finished meeting is extract-only, so you can read and download but never rewrite the record.

Leaving & review troubleshooting

No red End meeting button — You're not the host, organiser, co-host, or project authority

Only those roles can end a meeting for everyone. Use Leave to exit yourself; ask the host to end it. → 17.1

Meeting suddenly went read-only — The host ended the meeting

Expected. You're now in review — read the transcript, download it, or click Leave. Nothing you do can edit it. → 17.4

Can't type in chat — The meeting is finished (review mode)

Chat is read-only in a finished meeting by design. You can still read every message. → 17.5

Can't upload a file — Review mode disables the library

Uploads are off in a finished meeting — the library is read-only. Open existing files to view them. → 17.5

Summary tab is empty — No summary generated yet

Click Generate summary on the Summary tab. Hidden in review — read or download instead. → 17.6

“Couldn't end the meeting — check your network and try again.” — The finish write to the server failed (host action)

Nothing changed; the host can simply retry End meeting. → 17.4

Re-opened a meeting and it's read-only — The meeting is finished — it always opens for review

Expected. A finished meeting is immutable and extract-only every time you open it. → 17.4

Note

Leaving never loses work — your canvas is saved before the room closes. Ending is the only irreversible exit, and only a host can do it.
