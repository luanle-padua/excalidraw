Transcription & translation AI

The same AI that powers the live transcript and the translation tools — how it works, and where to control it.

Speech to text, and text to your language

The live transcript and the translate tools are AI-powered. Speech in the call is transcribed in real time into the transcript panel and the caption strip; selected text and chat messages are translated on demand into Vietnamese, English or Korean.

You drive both from the places they live — the transcript panel and the Translate controls in Communication. This chapter is just the explanation of what's doing the work.
