Part 5 · File management

Project-based materials

Files you keep across meetings — your personal “My Files” shelf — versus the files that belong to a single meeting's library.

Reuse a file from “My Files”

  1. Click From my shelf (the folder-heart button next to Upload).
  2. The My Files picker opens with the hint Click to copy into this meeting.
  3. Click any row to copy that document into the meeting; its row shows Copying into the meeting… while it works.
  4. If the file is marked Private, confirm the prompt that warns everyone in the meeting will be able to see it.
  5. The picker closes and the copied file appears as a normal library tile, owned by you.

→ The meeting gets its own private copy of the document — baked and encrypted for this room. Deleting it here never touches the original on your shelf.

Note

Each row shows the file kind, name, a Private or Sharable badge, and its size. If your shelf is empty you'll read “Your shelf is empty — upload from the home page, under “My Files”.”

Host-only

From my shelf only appears for internal accounts. If you do not see it, your account simply isn't connected to a personal shelf — use the upload button instead; the result is identical.

Personal shelf vs. the meeting library

Canvas M keeps materials in two places. Your My Files shelf is personal and persistent — upload a drawing once and reuse it in any meeting you're in. The meeting library (next chapter) is shared and scoped to one meeting: whatever's on the shelf there, everyone in that room can see and place.

Bringing a file from your shelf into a meeting copies it into that meeting's library — so editing it in the meeting never touches your personal original.
