My activities

A personal log of what you've done in Canvas M — meetings created, invitations answered, joins, and files uploaded.

Everything you've done, in one list

The My activities view (the History icon) keeps a running record of your own actions: meetings you created, invitations you received and how you replied, meetings you joined, and files you uploaded.

It's built from your existing data rather than a separate log, so it's always an honest reflection of what happened — handy for retracing “when did I last open that project?”
