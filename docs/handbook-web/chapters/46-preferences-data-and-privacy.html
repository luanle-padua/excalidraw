Note

Interface preferences (language, theme, captions) are kept on this device only. Every control here is the same one used inline elsewhere, so a change takes effect immediately — including mid-call, if your camera is already on. Background effects are desktop-only: on iPad or phone web the picker renders greyed out with the note Background effects are only supported on desktop. If an admin has lowered the max video quality, tiers above the cap are greyed and a note reads Admin caps the max at {level} — Auto always stays selectable and simply rides the cap.

Host-only

The video-quality cap shown here is set org-wide by an admin; you can pick any tier up to it but not above. There is no participant control to raise the cap.

Microphone consent and what we keep

The Privacy tab gives you a single, plain-language consent switch and a note describing the data the app holds. The toggle is labelled Allow mic transcription (Deepgram): when on, your mic audio is sent to Deepgram for live transcription; when off, nothing is sent.

This is the same on/off state as Enable mic for STT in Preferences — flipping it here also flips it there, and vice versa. Turning it off is an immediate, complete opt-out: no audio leaves your device for transcription.

What we store about you

The note reads: We store your profile (name, company, avatar), a log of meetings you join, files you upload, and transcripts while you have STT on. Interface preferences (language, theme, captions) are kept on this device only. Your My data tab lets you review the lists behind this — your meetings, invitations, and files — each read-only, with empty states (No meetings yet., No invitations yet., No files yet.) when there is nothing to show and Loading… while they fetch.
