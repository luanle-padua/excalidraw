Client waiting room

What a client sees when they join a live meeting, and how a host lets them in — the knock-to-join waiting room.

Letting a client into the room

A client who opens a live meeting they haven't been admitted to lands in a waiting room — “Waiting for the host to let you in…” — rather than walking straight in. A host admits or denies each knock from the Participants panel (covered in Host a meeting).

If a host denies the knock, the client sees “Not admitted” and can knock again after a short cooldown. Internal staff skip the waiting room and join directly.
