Part 12 · User dashboard

Backgrounds & avatar

Make the dashboard yours: pick a wallpaper for your lobby, and choose or upload the avatar that represents you in meetings.

Set your lobby wallpaper

  1. On your dashboard, find the small picture icon in the lobby header (next to the notification bell). Its label is Hình nền (“Wallpaper”).
  2. Click it. A small glass popover titled Hình nền opens with a grid of tiles.
  3. Click any tile to apply it instantly — the desk repaints behind the panes as you click. The active tile shows a highlighted border.
  4. To use your own picture, click the last tile, Tải ảnh lên (“Upload image”), and choose an image file. Canvas M resizes and compresses it before saving.
  5. To go back to the plain glass desk, click the Mặc định (“Default”) tile, or click the small × on your uploaded tile.
  6. Click anywhere outside the popover to close it.

→ Your chosen wallpaper paints behind the glass and is remembered on this browser. The image renders sharp — the frosted look comes from the glass panes in front of it, not from blurring the photo.

Note

Labels in this picker are in Vietnamese regardless of your interface language — this small lobby control is not yet translated.

The wallpaper choices

Default — the plain Glass-Desk gradient, no image.

Forest mist — a soft photographic preset.

Crystal leaves — a photographic preset.

Olive champagne — a dark low-chroma gradient.

Pastel day — a bright low-chroma gradient.

Upload your own — becomes “Ảnh của bạn” (Your image) once set, with an × to remove.

Pick a photographic preset for warmth, a gradient for a calm low-distraction desk, or upload a company image. The two gradients are tuned so the glass panes stay readable on top.

Set your name, company, and avatar

  1. Open Account settings and select the Profile tab (you usually land here).
  2. Type into Display name — placeholder e.g. Luan Le, up to 48 characters. The live preview and the Save button enable as soon as it is non-blank.
  3. Optionally fill Company — placeholder e.g. MAP Architects, also up to 48 characters.
  4. Pick a built-in tile from the Avatar gallery, or press ⬆ Upload image to choose your own picture.
  5. Press Save. The window stays open; there is no Cancel button here — the header Close is the dismiss.

→ Your new name, company, and avatar save to this device and broadcast to everyone in the meeting — your participant tile, cursor label, and the built-in avatar list all update at once. A library avatar (a lib: pick) also syncs to your account, so it follows your login to other browsers; an uploaded image stays on this device only.

Note

Display name is required: with the field blank the preview shows the placeholder and Save stays disabled. Leading and trailing spaces are trimmed before saving.

Host-only

Nothing here renames or removes other people — this tab only ever edits your own identity. Host controls for moderating participants live in the meeting, not in Settings.

Three ways to set your avatar

Eight built-in tiles. Click one to select it (a selected ring appears); it is stored as a lib: reference and syncs to your account across devices.

⬆ Upload image opens a file picker (images only). The picture is auto-resized to 256 px on its longest edge and kept on this device. A Remove button appears once an avatar is set.

With no avatar chosen — or after pressing Remove — your tile falls back to a generated avatar built from your name and email.

Use a gallery tile if you want the same face on every device you sign in from; upload an image when you want your real photo, accepting that it stays local to this browser.
