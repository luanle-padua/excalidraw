The basics

A one-page tour of everything you do in a meeting — so you know what's possible before the detailed chapters. Each capability links onward.

Everything you can do, in one breath

A Canvas M meeting is one shared, infinite canvas. You write and draw with the toolbar, drop notes, stickers and stamps, and chat down the side. You upload files — images, PDFs, DXF drawings and IFC models — and place them on the board for everyone. You turn on your microphone and camera, share your screen, and let live transcription and translation keep a record in your language.

Nothing here is one-way: every mark, page and model is shared live, and an AI can summarise the whole meeting when it ends. The chapters ahead take each of these in turn.
