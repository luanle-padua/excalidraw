On-canvas attribution

Canvas M has no separate comment threads — you discuss on the canvas itself. Every piece of text carries an author badge so the room always knows who wrote what.

See who wrote each piece of text

Canvas M stamps the creator's name onto every text element they add. An author badge — a small avatar plus a short name — floats above the top-left corner of authored text so you can tell at a glance who left it.

By default badges are quiet: one appears only over the text you have selected or are hovering. To reveal every author at once, click the people-shaped Show authors button in the toolbar; its tooltip flips to Hide authors while active. Click again to go back to the calm, selection-only view. Badges are deliberately text-only — shapes, images, stickers, and stamps don't get them, which keeps the canvas readable.

Bot answers carry a fixed identity: a 🤖 avatar and the MCM Bot name, so AI-generated text is always distinguishable from a person's.
Good to know

A badge survives its author leaving the room — the name is snapshotted onto the element, so it keeps showing even after that person disconnects. If a name can't be resolved, the badge falls back to Guest. Badges never appear on text bound inside a container (like the bot frame's own label), because the frame already shows who it belongs to.
