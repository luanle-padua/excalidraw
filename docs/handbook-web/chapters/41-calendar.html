Calendar

The month calendar on your dashboard, with Korean and Vietnamese public holidays painted in.

A calendar that knows the holidays

The dashboard calendar lays your meetings out across the month and lets you create one on any day. It also paints the working week: Sundays and public holidays in red, Saturdays in blue, with Korean and Vietnamese public holidays fetched automatically for each year.

Country-aware timing — showing a meeting in each attendee's own timezone — is planned but not yet available; for now times show in your own local time.
