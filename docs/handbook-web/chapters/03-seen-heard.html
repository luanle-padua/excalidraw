Be seen & heard

Joining a Canvas M meeting never forces your microphone on. You enter as a quiet listener, then choose — one button at a time — when to speak, when to show your camera, when to raise your hand, and when to react. This chapter walks every one of those controls, exactly as they appear in the meeting header, and every state they can show.

Join the call (listener-first)

  1. Open the meeting. The media controls live in the meeting header, not on the canvas, so the drawing surface stays clear.
  2. Find the open-door icon labelled Join. This is the only call button visible before you connect.
  3. Click Join. No microphone prompt appears — you join listening-only.
  4. The button becomes a spinner with the tooltip “Requesting mic permission…” while it connects.
  5. Once connected, the spinner is replaced by the full live cluster: mic, camera, raise-hand, reactions, recording, and a red Leave call button.

→ You are now in the audio/video call as a listener. Your microphone has not been touched yet, and your camera is off. Nothing you do is broadcast until you flip a control on.

Note

Joining the call is separate from joining the meeting. You can sit on the canvas without ever clicking Join — you simply won't hear or be heard until you do.

Three exits, one entrance

The single most common confusion in any meeting tool is which button leaves what. Canvas M deliberately gives every exit a distinct glyph so they never collide.

The red phone-down Leave call button (in the media cluster) hangs up only the audio/video call — you stay in the meeting and on the canvas, and can re-Join at any time. Walking out of the meeting entirely is a separate door icon (Leave) further along the header. Ending the meeting for everyone is a third, host-only control (End meeting). Three actions, three icons, no overlap. The open door you click to enter is intentionally not a microphone icon, because entering no longer promises that your mic turns on.

Host-only

End meeting closes the room for all participants and switches everyone to read-only review. As a participant you will never see it — only the host does.

Speak when you're ready

  1. In the live cluster, find the microphone icon. Right after you join it shows “Unmute” on hover — you are connected but silent.
  2. Click it once. The browser asks for microphone permission now (this first click is what acquires the mic), then your audio goes live and the tooltip flips to “Mute”.
  3. Click again to mute. The icon switches to a struck-through mic, turns red, and the tooltip reads “Unmute”.
  4. Watch your own avatar tile in the people strip: a green dot means your mic is live; a red mic-off badge means you're muted.

→ Your mic toggles between live and muted with a single click. The permission prompt only ever fires on the very first unmute — a deliberate user gesture — never on join.

Note

If your device has no microphone at all, the mic button is disabled and its tooltip reads “This device has no microphone — listen only”. You can still hear everyone; you simply can't transmit.

Host-only

A host can mute you from the participants panel. If that happens your mic button shows muted and you'll need to unmute yourself again to speak.

Reading the microphone button

No mic on this device. Button disabled, struck-through mic, tooltip “This device has no microphone — listen only”. You hear everyone.

Solid mic icon, tooltip “Mute”. You're transmitting; your tile shows a green live dot.

Struck-through mic painted red, tooltip “Unmute”. Your tile shows a red mic-off badge.

Glance here whenever you're unsure whether you're being heard — the icon shape, its colour, and the tooltip together tell you the exact state.

Turn on your camera

  1. Find the camera icon next to the mic. It starts off — a struck-through camera with the tooltip “Turn on camera”.
  2. Click it. While the camera spins up, the icon becomes a brief spinner.
  3. Once live, the icon becomes a solid camera (highlighted as active) and the tooltip reads “Turn off camera”. Your avatar tile is replaced by your live video, mirrored for a natural self-view.
  4. Click again to turn the camera off and return to your avatar.

→ Your camera is opt-in and defaults to off. If your video fails to attach, your tile falls back to your avatar rather than showing a black box.

Note

If this device has no camera, the button is disabled with the tooltip “No camera detected on this device”. If the camera can't start (permissions or busy), the tooltip reads “Couldn't start the camera — check permissions and retry.” and you stay on your avatar.

Good to know

Virtual / blurred backgrounds aren't on the call bar — they live in Settings → Preferences. Your saved choice auto-applies whenever your camera turns on.

Raise your hand & react

  1. To raise your hand, click the hand icon — tooltip “Raise hand”. It highlights, and a ✋ badge appears on your avatar for everyone to see.
  2. Click it again (now “Lower hand”) to put it down. Leaving the call lowers it automatically.
  3. To react, click the smiley icon — tooltip “Reactions” — to open the emoji popover.
  4. Pick from 👍 ❤️ 😂 🎉 👏 😮. The emoji floats up over your avatar for everyone and fades after a few seconds.
  5. Press Esc or click outside to close the popover without sending anything.

→ Your raised hand is sticky and visible to all peers; a reaction is a brief, animated burst. Both broadcast in real time without you having to unmute.

Note

Raise-hand and reactions are participant gestures — there is no separate host approval. Reactions never make sound; they're purely visual.

Reading the room
  1. 1 People strip — every participant as an avatar (or live video) tile. The chip on the left shows joined / invited and how many are in call.
  2. 2 Your own tile (“You”) is highlighted. Click it to open Your profile (name, company, avatar).
  3. 3 Mic dot / badge — green dot = live, red struck-mic = muted, dimmed tile = not in the call yet.
  4. 4 Crown marks the host; a quieter half-crown marks a Co-host. A 📺 badge means that person is sharing their screen.
  5. 5 ✋ raised-hand and floating reaction emojis appear over the relevant avatar.
  6. 6 Click any other person's tile to follow their view (your viewport tracks theirs); press Esc to stop.

Microphone & camera fixes

Mic button is greyed out, tooltip says “listen only” — No microphone on this device, so you joined listener-only.

You can still hear everyone. Plug in a mic / headset and rejoin to speak. → 3.4

Error icon on the call bar after Join — Mic permission denied.

Tooltip reads “Microphone denied — enable it in browser settings and retry.” Allow the mic in your browser, then click the icon to retry. → 3.4

Can't unmute — error tooltip mentions another app — Your mic is held by Teams / Zoom or similar.

“Microphone is in use by another app (Teams / Zoom). Quit it and retry.” Close the other app and click to retry. → 3.4

Camera button disabled — No camera detected on this device.

Tooltip: “No camera detected on this device.” Connect a webcam, then rejoin. → 3.6

Camera won't turn on — Permission blocked or device busy.

“Couldn't start the camera — check permissions and retry.” Grant camera access in the browser and click again; your avatar shows meanwhile. → 3.6

Whole call bar is missing — You're reviewing a finished meeting (read-only).

There's no live call here. Open the live meeting link to join a call. → 3.9

Couldn't join — network message — The call failed to connect.

“Couldn't join the call — check your network and try again.” Click the error icon to retry. → 3.2

Note

After any failed join the call button becomes a single red error icon — clicking it retries. The plain-English headline rides the hover tooltip; the raw technical detail is on the native title for support.
