Laser pointer

Point without leaving a mark. The laser is a temporary dot every participant sees, ideal for walking the room through a drawing.

Point at the canvas without drawing on it

Pick the laser pointer from the toolbar and your cursor becomes a glowing dot that everyone in the room sees in real time. It's perfect for “look here” — walking people through a plan or a model without adding anything permanent.

Unlike a pen stroke, the laser leaves nothing behind: the trail fades as you move and disappears when you switch tools, so the canvas stays clean.
