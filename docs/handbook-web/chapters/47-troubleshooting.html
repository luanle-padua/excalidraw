Troubleshooting

A mic that won't unmute, a camera that won't start, a waiting room that won't let you in. Most snags in Canvas M show their own fix right on the button. This chapter reads the exact words the app shows you, names the cause, and gives you the one move that clears it.

The app tells you what's wrong

Canvas M rarely fails silently. When a control can't do its job, it changes shape, turns colour, or carries a short message you can read by resting your pointer on it. Before you reach for a reload, read what the button is already telling you.

Three places carry the truth. Tooltips appear when you hover an icon in the meeting header — the mic, camera, and call buttons all describe their current state and what a click will do. Toasts slide in briefly for one-off failures, such as a screen-share that wouldn't start. Full-screen cards take over when you can't enter a room at all — the start gate and the waiting room each explain why and offer the next step. Match the wording you see to the tables in this chapter; the exact text is the key.

Read the button before you reload it.
Where messages surface Header tooltips for live controls, toasts for transient errors, and full-screen gate cards for entry blocks.
Note

Many controls that look broken are simply Host-only. If a button is greyed with a tooltip naming someone else — for example “{host} is recording the meeting” or “{name} is presenting — wait for them to finish” — nothing is wrong; that action belongs to the host or the current presenter.

Microphone won't work

Mic icon is red and tooltip reads “This device has no microphone — listen only” — No microphone exists on this device, so you joined in listen-only mode. You can hear everyone but can't speak.

Plug in or enable a microphone, then leave and rejoin the call. On a phone, check that a headset is connected. → callControls.listenOnlyTitle

Tooltip reads “Microphone denied — enable it in browser settings and retry.” — You blocked the mic permission, so the browser refused access (a NotAllowedError).

Open your browser's site settings, allow the microphone for this site, then click the red mic button to Retry. → callControls.micDenied

Tooltip reads “Microphone is in use by another app (Teams / Zoom). Quit it and retry.” — Another program has grabbed the mic exclusively (a NotReadableError).

Close Teams, Zoom, or any other call app, then click the mic button to Retry. → callControls.micBusy

Tooltip reads “Requesting mic permission…” and the button shows a spinner — The browser's permission prompt is open. Canvas M only asks for the mic the first time you unmute or turn on speech-to-text — not when you join.

Answer the browser prompt with Allow. The spinner clears as soon as you respond. → callControls.requestingMic

Tooltip reads “Could not start microphone” with a click-to-retry hint — A generic mic error the browser didn't classify. The raw detail rides the native tooltip.

Click the red mic button to Retry. If it persists, reload the page and rejoin. → callControls.cannotStartMic

Live transcript shows “No local mic stream — this device is in listen-only mode.” — You turned on speech-to-text but have no working mic, so there is nothing to transcribe.

Give the mic permission (or attach a mic) and unmute. Speech-to-text follows your live mic once it's flowing. → stt.micErrorNoStream

Note

Joining is deliberately mic-free: clicking Join enters listening-only and the browser asks for the mic only on your first unmute. That later click is itself the gesture the browser needs to show its prompt.

Every call-button state, at a glance

Open-door icon. You're in the room but not in the audio/video call yet. Click to join listening-only.

Accent mic = you can speak; click to Mute. Red MicOff = muted or listen-only.

No mic on this device. The mic button is disabled and labelled listen-only; you still hear everyone.

Single red MicOff button. The tooltip names the cause; clicking it retries the join.

A disabled spinner button while the call hooks up or the mic prompt is open.

VideoOff by default; click to turn the camera on. A spinner shows while it starts.

Use this to identify which state your call button is in before matching a row in the tables — the icon and colour tell you as much as the tooltip.

Camera won't turn on

Camera button is greyed and tooltip reads “No camera detected on this device” — Canvas M probed for a camera when the call went live and found none, so the toggle is disabled.

Connect a webcam, then leave and rejoin the call so the device is re-probed. The audio call keeps working without a camera. → callControls.noCameraTitle

Tooltip reads “Couldn't start the camera — check permissions and retry.” — The camera exists but failed to start — usually a denied permission or another app holding it.

Allow the camera in your browser's site settings (or close the other app), then click the camera button again. → callControls.cameraError

Camera button shows a spinner and won't respond — The camera is in its starting state. The toggle is disabled until it resolves to on or off.

Wait a moment. If it never settles, leave and rejoin the call. → videoState.CameraStatus

Background blur or image options are missing or greyed — Background effects are desktop-only. The tooltip reads “Background effects are only supported on desktop”.

Use a laptop or desktop to change or blur your camera background. Your saved choice auto-applies when the camera turns on. → videoBg.desktopOnlyTitle

Note

Turning the camera on never disturbs your audio: it opts in on the same call, defaulting to off. If permission is denied the tile simply keeps showing your avatar.

Can't get into the meeting

Lobby shows “That link or room code isn't valid.” — The link or code you pasted into Paste a link or room code to join didn't parse as a meeting.

Re-copy the full invite link and paste it again. Ask the host to resend if it still fails. → lobby.joinError

Full-screen card: “Meeting hasn't started yet” with a spinner and “Waiting for the host to start the meeting…” — You opened a scheduled meeting before the host pressed start. Canvas M polls and joins you automatically the moment it goes live.

Leave the card open and wait — you'll be pulled in as soon as it starts. Use Back to home if you'd rather wait elsewhere. → gate.waitingHost

Card: “Waiting to be let in” with “Waiting for the host to let you in…” — As an external guest, you knocked on a live meeting with a waiting room. Internal staff skip this; guests wait to be admitted.

Wait — Canvas M polls your knock and admits you automatically once the host accepts. It shows who you're “Joining as” while you wait. → gate.waitingAdmit

Card: “Not admitted” with “The host hasn't let you in right now. You can knock again in a moment.” — The host denied your knock, or you're inside the 30-second re-knock cooldown.

Click Knock again. If it doesn't take, wait a few seconds for the cooldown and retry; the button reads “Knocking…” while it sends. → gate.deniedDesc

Card: “Meeting cancelled” — “The organizer cancelled this meeting.” — The organizer cancelled this meeting; there is nothing live to join.

Click Back to home. Watch your invitations for a rescheduled time. → gate.cancelledDesc

Card: “Meeting has ended” — “This meeting has ended. The organizer will share a recap with you separately.” — The meeting finished. Internal members open it read-only for review; guests see only this notice.

Nothing to do — wait for the host's recap. Internal members can reopen it from the project for read-only review. → gate.finishedDesc

Banner: “The host removed you from the meeting.” — The host removed you from the room.

Nothing in-app overrides this. If it was a mistake, ask the host to re-invite you. → participants.kickedMsg

Error toast: “Couldn't verify the meeting state (lost connection to the server). Try again later.” — Canvas M lost its connection to the server while checking whether the meeting could be joined.

Check your network and try again later. A reload re-runs the check from a clean state. → errors.joinUnverified

Host-only

The Start meeting button and the admit / deny waiting-room controls are Host-only — they only appear for the owning department (organizer, host, co-host, or a same-department member). If you don't see Start meeting, you're not expected to; simply wait.

Screen sharing won't start

Present button is greyed with tooltip “Screen sharing isn't supported on this device or browser (e.g. iPad / iPhone). Present from a computer instead.” — Your browser doesn't offer the screen-capture API. iPad and iPhone Safari can't share a screen at all.

Present from a computer instead. Open the same meeting link on a laptop or desktop to share. → header.presentUnsupported

Present button is disabled while someone else is sharing — Only one person presents at a time. The presenter's tooltip reads “{name} is presenting — wait for them to finish”.

Wait for the current presenter to stop. The button frees up the moment they end their share. → screenShare.lockedBy

Toast: “Couldn't present your screen.” (with extra detail) — The browser's screen-picker was cancelled or the capture failed to start.

Click Present again and pick a window or screen in the browser dialog. Don't dismiss the picker. → errors.presentFailed

Toast: “Couldn't share your screen” — The screen track errored after it started.

Stop and restart the share. If it keeps failing, reload and rejoin the call before presenting. → screenShare.startError

Shared screen shows “Connecting…” and doesn't appear — The presenter's screen track is still being negotiated.

Wait a moment — it resolves on its own. If it never paints, ask the presenter to stop and re-share. → screenShare.connecting

Note

When you do present, you'll see You are presenting; others see {name} is presenting. Use Pop out to drag the shared view to a second screen, or Stop sharing to end it.

A file won't open or upload

Toast: “Only images, DXF, PDF and IFC are supported for now. Skipped: {name}” — You dropped a file type the room library doesn't handle. Unsupported files are skipped.

Convert to an image, PDF, DXF, or IFC and upload again. The upload bar lists the accepted types. → library.unsupportedType

Toast: “Couldn't process IFC file: {name}” — The IFC model couldn't be parsed — usually a corrupt or unsupported export.

Re-export the IFC from your BIM tool and upload again. → library.ifcProcessFailed

3D pane shows “Couldn't read IFC model” or CAD pane shows “Couldn't read DXF” — The viewer failed to load the file's geometry. The detail follows the message.

Close the pane and reopen the file. If it still fails, re-export and re-upload it. → ifc.status.loadError

Pane reads “Maximum 3D models already open. Close another file to view this one.” (or the DXF equivalent) — You've hit the limit of simultaneously open 3D/CAD viewers.

Close an open IFC or DXF tab, then open the new one. → ifc.status.capacity

Viewer sits on “Waiting for IFC file from peer…” (also for DXF / PDF) — Another participant opened the file; your copy is still streaming over from them.

Wait — it loads once the transfer completes. If it stalls, ask them to re-open it. → ifc.status.waitingPeer

Deleting a library file warns “This file is locked by {lockedBy}. Ask them to unlock it first.” — Someone locked the file to stop accidental deletion.

Ask the person who locked it (or the original uploader) to unlock it, then delete. → library.deleteLockedAlert

My Files: “Upload failed: {name}” or “File too large (max 50MB): {name}” — The upload to your personal shelf failed, or the file exceeds the 50MB limit.

Check your connection and retry; for oversized files, compress or split below 50MB. → myfiles.uploadFailed

Picker: “Couldn't load the file from your shelf: {name}” — Copying a shelf file into the meeting failed mid-transfer.

Try the file again from My Files. A reload of the meeting also clears a stuck copy. → myfiles.copyFailed

Note

If a meeting has finished and you reopen it for review, the library turns read-only: “The meeting has finished — the library is read-only, uploads are disabled.” That's expected, not a fault.

Captions or transcript aren't appearing

Transcript panel reads “Turn on STT to start speech recognition.” with a PAUSED badge — Speech-to-text is off. Captions and the live transcript both depend on it.

Toggle STT: on in the transcript panel. The badge changes from PAUSED to LIVE. → stt.paused

Badge reads No audio even though STT is on — STT is on but no audio is reaching it — usually a muted mic, or a suspended audio context on iPad.

Unmute your mic. On iPad, tap the page once to wake the audio context. → stt.statusNoAudio

Transcript shows “Waiting for someone to speak…” and never fills — STT is live but no one is speaking, or no mic audio is being captured.

If you expect captions of your own voice, confirm your mic is unmuted and the badge reads Live. → stt.waiting

Panel shows “STT error” or “STT WebSocket error” (badge ERROR) — The connection to the speech service dropped or failed to open.

Toggle STT off and on to reconnect. If it persists, reload and rejoin the call. → stt.wsError

Panel reads “Failed to load STT worklet: {message}” — The audio-processing module the browser needs for STT failed to load.

Reload the page so the worklet re-downloads, then turn STT on again. → stt.workletLoadFailed

Meeting log is empty: “No transcript yet. Join a call + turn on STT to start recording.” — No segments were captured — either no one had STT on, or the call was never joined.

Join the audio call and turn on STT so future speech is recorded into the log. → log.emptyTranscript

Captions don't show even with STT on — The caption strip can be hidden, or set to auto-show only while presenting.

Turn on Captions (CC) in the header (it toggles between “Turn on captions (CC)” and “Turn off captions (CC)”). Check Show captions while presenting in Settings → Preferences. → header.captionToggle

Note

Speech-to-text needs your consent. In Settings → Privacy, Allow mic transcription (Deepgram) must be on; off means nothing from your mic is sent and you'll never see your own captions.

Badge / labelWhere it showsWhat it means
LIVETranscript panelSpeech-to-text is on and the session is connected.

No audioTranscript panelSTT is on but no audio is reaching it — muted mic or suspended context.

TESTTranscript panelYou're running an audio file through STT to check accuracy, not a live mic.

Note

Badges are status, not errors — most need no action. Only ERROR and No audio ask you to do something.

Host-only

Recording carries its own badges: a non-host sees “{host} is recording the meeting” and the record button stays disabled. Starting and stopping a recording is Host-only.
