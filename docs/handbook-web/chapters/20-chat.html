Part 6 · Communication

Chat

The Conversation panel is the room's running text channel. Type a line, mention a file, summon the AI, react with a tap, quote a reply, and read every message in your own language — all from one quiet, two-row composer beside the canvas.

The panel at a glance
  1. 1 Header — the title Conversation on the left, and on the right the auto-translate toggle.
  2. 2 Auto-translate toggle — the globe button. Its tooltip reads Auto-translate (on) or Auto-translate (off) depending on state; it tints when active.
  3. 3 Message stream — messages grouped by author. The first message in a group carries the avatar, name and time; later messages from the same person within three minutes are bare bubbles indented beneath.
  4. 4 Your own messages — labelled You and styled distinctly from peers' bubbles.
  5. 5 Composer — a two-row card: a one-line text area on top that grows as you type, and an action row below.
  6. 6 Action row — left to right: the emoji (smiley) button, the Mention file / bot (@) button, the Attach image (paperclip) button, then the Send button on the right.

Send a message

  1. Click the text area — its placeholder reads Type a message….
  2. Type your line. The card highlights once there is non-blank text, and the Send button enables.
  3. Press Enter to send. Use Shift+Enter for a line break without sending.
  4. Or click the Send (paper-plane) button on the right of the action row.

→ Your message appears at the bottom of the stream under your avatar and the label You, and the panel scrolls to it. Empty or whitespace-only drafts are ignored — Send stays disabled until you type something.

Note

Messages are sent end-to-end encrypted with the room key. The stream auto-scrolls to the newest message whenever the count changes, so you always land on the latest line.

When the panel is quiet

Before you join a live collaboration the panel shows a chat icon, the title Start a live collaboration session, and the subtitle Messages are end-to-end encrypted with the room key. There is no composer yet.

In a live room with an empty history you see No messages yet and the hint Type @ to mention a file, @bot to ask AI. The composer is ready below.

Open a finished meeting for review and the history is visible but frozen. The composer is replaced by the notice Reviewing a finished meeting — chat is read-only. Reactions and replies are disabled too.

Read these states as signals: an encryption subtitle means you haven't started collaborating yet; the read-only notice means the meeting is closed and extract-only.

Reference a file from the library

  1. Type @ anywhere in your draft, or click the Mention file / bot (@) button to insert one and open the picker.
  2. Keep typing to filter — the picker lists up to eight matching library files by name, plus the MCM Bot option at the top.
  3. Use ↑ / ↓ to move the highlight, then Enter or Tab to insert; or click an entry. Press Esc to dismiss the picker.
  4. The file is inserted as a coloured mention chip in your message.
  5. In the sent message, click the mention chip — its tooltip reads Click to scroll to file on canvas — to fly the canvas to that image.

→ Recipients see the same chip. Clicking it scrolls and zooms to the matching image on the canvas.

Note

If the picker finds nothing it shows No files in this room's library yet. when the library is empty, or No files match "…" with your query when none match. If you click a mention whose image isn't placed yet, an alert explains: This file isn't on the canvas yet. Open the library and click it to place first.

Tapback reactions and quoted replies

  1. Hover any message (your own or a peer's) to reveal a floating reaction bar above the bubble.
  2. Click an emoji — the set is 👍 ❤️ 😂 😮 🎉 🔥 👀 🙏 💯 ✅ 🤔 😢 — to attach it. Clicking the same emoji again removes your reaction.
  3. Reactions appear as a pill under the bubble with a running count; the tooltip reads {n} people. Your own reactions are highlighted.
  4. To reply, click the curved-arrow Reply button at the right end of that same hover bar (tooltip Reply to this message).
  5. A quote banner appears above the composer showing the author and a snippet; type your answer and send. Press Esc or the × to cancel the reply.
  6. Sent replies show the quoted snippet above the bubble — click it (tooltip Scroll to original message) to jump to and briefly flash the original.

→ Reactions and replies broadcast to everyone in the room in real time.

Note

The bot's messages can't be reacted to, and the whole hover bar is hidden while you're reviewing a finished meeting.

Note

Only images are accepted as attachments. The action row spaces the emoji, mention and attach buttons on the left and the Send button on the right.

Host-only

There is no participant control to clear, delete or moderate chat history. The transcript and library content the bot draws on are governed by the meeting's lifecycle, not by individual participants.

Chat troubleshooting

No composer, just an encryption message — You haven't started a live collaboration session yet

Start or join a live session; the composer appears once you're in the room → 13.2

Composer replaced by a read-only notice — You opened a finished meeting for review

Finished meetings are immutable — history is extract-only, no new messages, reactions or replies → 13.2

Attach skipped my file — Only image files are supported

An alert reads "Only images are supported. Skipped: …". Re-attach an image (PNG/JPG/etc.) → 13.ref

Mention chip does nothing / shows an alert — The referenced image isn't placed on the canvas yet

"This file isn't on the canvas yet. Open the library and click it to place first." → 13.3

@bot apologised instead of answering — The AI had no useful answer, or the service/connection failed

Try again later; check your connection. Make sure your question is clear and follows @bot → 13.bot

Messages still in another language — Auto-translate is off, or the message is already in your language

Click the globe toggle until its tooltip reads "Auto-translate (on)" → 13.translate

Note

If a translation seems stuck on "Translating…", it's fetching in the background — it resolves once the result returns or falls back to the original.
