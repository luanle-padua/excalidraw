Canvas AI

What the AI actually is in Canvas M — and what it does and doesn't do on your board.

The AI reads the meeting and writes back text

Canvas M's on-canvas intelligence is the MCM Bot. It reads the live meeting — the chat, the transcript and the text on the canvas — and writes its answer back as a real, shared canvas element everyone can see and move.

It is a text assistant: it summarises, lists decisions, and answers questions. It does not draw shapes, arrange your layout, or generate models — so treat it as a smart note-taker on the board, not an auto-designer.
