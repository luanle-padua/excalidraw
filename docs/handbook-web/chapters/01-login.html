Part 1 · Quick start

Login

Canvas M has one front door: sign in with your email and password. If someone sent you a meeting link, paste it first and it carries you straight into the room once you've signed in.

The login card
  1. 1 Login — the card heading, with “Sign in to your account.” beneath it and the “Architectural collaboration platform” tagline above.
  2. 2 Email and Password fields — placeholders you@company.com and Enter your password.
  3. 3 Sign In — the submit button. It stays disabled until both fields have something, and reads Signing in… while it works.
  4. 4 Have a meeting link? — a small toggle below the form for pasting a room link before you sign in.
  5. 5 Need help? Contact your administrator. — the footer. There's no self-service sign-up or password reset.

Sign in with email and password

  1. Type your work email in the Email field (placeholder you@company.com).
  2. Type your password in the Password field (placeholder Enter your password). Everyone signs in this way — internal staff and invited clients alike.
  3. Click Sign In. While it checks, the button reads Signing in…; on success the card disappears and your dashboard loads.
  4. There's no “sign up” — your administrator creates the account and gives you the password. The footer reads “Need help? Contact your administrator.”

→ Your session is remembered on this device until you sign out, so you won't retype your password every visit.

Note

Password is the only way in right now. A one-time login link by email exists in the product but is switched off until it works reliably on every network — so don't look for an email-link option on the card.

Came in on a meeting link?

  1. If a host sent you a meeting link, click Have a meeting link? under the sign-in form.
  2. Paste the link — or its bare ID,KEY — into the Meeting link field (placeholder Paste meeting link or ID,KEY) and click Continue.
  3. Canvas M saves it and shows “Meeting link saved — sign in to join.”, then returns you to the password form.
  4. Sign in with your email and password as usual; you drop straight into that meeting once you're in.

→ The meeting link only carries you to the room — it doesn't replace signing in. Everyone still authenticates with a password first.

Note

If the link is malformed you'll see “That meeting link looks invalid.” — re-copy the whole link (or the ID,KEY) from your invitation.
