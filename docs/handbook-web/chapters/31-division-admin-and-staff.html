Division admin & staff

How authority works above a single project: department heads (“Division admin”) and the internal staff directory that project membership is drawn from.

Department heads and the staff directory

Above per-project roles sits the organisation. A department (“division”) head is the Division admin — the supreme authority on every project of their department. They can assign a project's leader and delegate co-operators, even on projects they aren't a member of. It's an org-level role read from the directory, separate from the per-project Leader / Co-operator / Participant roles.

Project membership itself is drawn only from internal staff accounts — the company directory. External people never become members; they're handled as clients (see the Clients part).
