Before you start · The mental model

What the waiting room is for

Some rooms open straight away; others ask you to knock. The waiting room is simply a held space — the host sees your name and lets you in when the meeting is ready for you. Nothing you do there is visible to the room, and you always arrive muted.

Think of it as the corridor outside a studio: you can hear nothing through the door, and nobody inside hears you. The moment the host admits you, your microphone is still off — you choose when to speak.

You never enter a meeting already talking. The first word is always yours to give.

Note If no one answers within ~30 seconds, you can knock again — your place is not lost.

Task · 2 min

Join with a link or code

  1. Open the invite link, or click Join on your dashboard.
  2. If asked, type the 6-digit code from your invite.
  3. Choose whether to turn on your camera — your microphone stays off until you unmute.
  4. Press Join meeting.

You're in, muted, and ready.

Heads up

Closing the tab cancels your knock — keep it open while you wait.

Host-only Admitting people and locking the room are the host's controls — you won't see them as a participant.
